Invisible Watermarks runs local pixel-watermark decoders when optional decoder packages are installed. It does not call Google, OpenAI, AWS, or other external portals.

The first supported local checks are open DWT-DCT watermarks used by some Stable Diffusion / SDXL / FLUX pipelines and Adobe TrustMark. TrustMark marks Content Credentials provenance, not AI generation by itself.

Optional decoders are disabled by default. A server operator must set INVISIBLE_WATERMARK_DECODERS=imwatermark,trustmark or INVISIBLE_WATERMARK_DECODERS=all after installing the matching packages.

Important limitation: These checks are positive-only. A local hit is useful evidence, but a miss does not mean the image is clean; many invisible watermarks are proprietary, fragile, or unavailable to local decoders.
