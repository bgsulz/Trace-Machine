Image files carry metadata: EXIF camera fields, XMP and IPTC tags, and PNG text chunks. Some AI tools write their prompt, settings, or an "AI-generated" label there. Today that's mostly local and open-source tools like ComfyUI, plus AI-label standards such as IPTC's digital source type and China's TC260. Mainstream generators now rely on C2PA and invisible watermarks instead.

Important limitation: Metadata is trivial to add, edit, or strip, so it's a clue, not proof, and missing metadata proves nothing.
