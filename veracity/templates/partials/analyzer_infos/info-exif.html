Image files can carry metadata in several places, including EXIF camera fields, PNG text chunks, XMP packets, and IPTC photo metadata. It's the reason you can use your photos app to see when photos were taken, what camera settings were used, and other technical details.

AI tools and image-generation interfaces sometimes write prompts, workflow data, generator names, or "Made with AI" labels into those metadata fields. Trace Machine checks for unsigned metadata clues from tools like Automatic1111, ComfyUI, Midjourney, NovelAI, xAI/Grok, and AI-label standards such as IPTC and TC260.

Important limitation: It's really easy to add, edit, or remove image metadata. You can do it in Photoshop, GIMP, or any number of online tools. So metadata is a clue, not proof, and the absence of metadata doesn't prove anything.
