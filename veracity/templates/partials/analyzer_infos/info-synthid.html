Verification Portals records manual reports from external provider checkers. These portals can see proprietary signals that Veracity cannot decode locally.

Google exposes checks through Google reverse image search and About this image. OpenAI exposes checks through OpenAI Verify. Meta exposes its checker through Meta Identify. These tools are provider-specific: a negative result from one portal does not rule out a watermark from another provider.

Important limitation: Portal reports are community evidence. A negative report means a user did not get a positive result from the portals they checked; it is not proof that no invisible watermark exists.
